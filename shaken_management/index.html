Sen Sulu: Strategic Analysis

Sen Sulu is a Kazakhstani decorative cosmetics brand founded in Astana in 2023 by Ainur Kabeldinova. It follows an accessible luxury concept: quality cosmetics, attractive packaging, and affordable prices. This report uses SWOT, Porter's Five Forces and the BCG matrix, plus a survey of 152 respondents, to find opportunities for the brand's future development.
